Read-only design audit · Red1218/finance_tracker_mobile @ main

Finance Tracker Mobile

Nothing in the repository was written, renamed, or committed — I read it over the GitHub API into a separate workspace, so the working tree is untouched by construction. Everything below is derived from the code that actually ships, not from the docs' aspirations. Where the two disagree, I say so.

What ships

Expo Router with one (tabs) group of nine routes behind a five-destination bottom bar, an auth gate at /auth, twelve feature modules under src/features, Supabase with RLS migrations, thirteen shared primitives, a light/dark token theme restored from user preferences at boot.

Documented, not built

The registry lists fifteen approved components; eight of them — AppBar, BottomNavigation, FAB, StatisticCard, NetWorthHero, BudgetOverviewCard, FinancialInsightCard, UpcomingBillsCard — carry ⏳ Implementation. KPICardsSection, QuickActionsSection and CategoryBreakdownSection exist and are tested but are not mounted in DashboardView. The shared AppBar is never used on the dashboard either.

Why it reads bland

Every dashboard section is the same Card variant="elevated" at the same radius, the same 16–20px padding and the same 24px/700 heading. Three stacked cards of equal weight means no hero — the eye has nowhere to land. That is a hierarchy problem, not a color problem.

Frozen, on purpose

The five-tab IA, the FAB→/spends?openModal contract, the semantic status colors, tabular numerals, the 44px touch floor and the section-level loading/error/empty container all stay. None of the proposals below moves logic into presentation or crosses a feature boundary.

What the light theme needs. lightColors is fully defined and, as far as the audit found, never exercised — and it will not survive a straight token swap. Three things change beyond the values: the accent must darken to oklch(0.52 0.13 290) ≈ #6b5cc4 to clear 4.5:1 on #F8FAFC (it measures 5.1:1; the dark-ground blurple manages only 3.1:1 there); the FAB inverts to a filled accent with white glyph, because a 1px outline on white has no presence; and the warning ramp must darken to oklch(0.62 0.12 75) on the light ground — amber on white is the worst pair in the palette, and the dark-theme value clears neither the 3:1 your §18 requires of graphical objects nor anything close to it. Surfaces work in reverse too — on the dark ground elevation is a lighter tint, on the light ground it is pure white plus a hairline. Boundaries: none — this is themeResolution, which already exists and is tested. Risk: low for the mock, medium to ship: nothing in the app has been seen in light mode, so expect a long tail of one-off hardcoded colors surfacing.

Preserve

The five-destination bottom bar and its route map (spends→Transactions, insights→Analytics), with Accounts / Categories / Finances / Settings collapsed under More — nine routes behind five tabs is the right call and the mapping is already explicit in (tabs)/_layout.tsx. The FAB → /spends?openModal=true single entry point for creating a transaction. SectionStateContainer: per-section skeleton / retry / empty instead of one page-level spinner is genuinely good and rare. Tabular numerals, the true minus, 44px minimum targets, and every semantic status color.

Evidence-backed problems

1. Flat hierarchy on Home — three equal elevated cards, two equal 24/700 headings, no hero. 2. surfaceElevated #334155 is used for the card, the ring track, the row badge and the pill, so elevation stops meaning anything. 3. Built and tested components sit unmounted (KPICardsSection, CategoryBreakdownSection, QuickActionsSection) while the registry marks eight approved ones ⏳ — the system's map no longer matches the territory. 4. Hardcoded hexes in KPICardsSection (rgba(22,163,74,.12)), QuickActionsSection and BudgetHealthSection's itemBorder violate §19 of your own design system. 5. colors.focus is defined and never used. 6. BudgetHealthSection colors its bar error at ≥80%, but MonthlyBudgetCard colors the ring warning at the same threshold — the same state, two colors.

Architecture impact

All four mockups are implementable without touching a boundary. They change composition inside presentation/ only: which sections DashboardView mounts, and how they are styled. The two derived figures (spend pace, days remaining) belong on the existing budget view-model in application/, formatted there and consumed as strings — the same contract BudgetHealthRow already uses. No new cross-feature imports; 1c's reuse of the Reporting segmented control means promoting it to src/shared/components, not importing across features. The Boundary test in __tests__/architecture should keep passing.

Readiness

Ready to implement: 1b hierarchy pass; 1d sign-in; replacing the hardcoded rgba values with tokens; wiring colors.focus; reconciling the 80% threshold color. Needs a design decision: ring vs. bar as the budget's primary form (1b vs 1c); whether the accent stays #2563EB or moves to the Nocturne blurple you picked for these mockups. Needs a product decision: dropping Recent Activity from Home; whether "Forgot password" ships or the affordance is removed. Needs architecture review: promoting the segmented control to shared; adding derived pace fields to the budget read model.
