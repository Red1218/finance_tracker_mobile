# Finance Tracker v2: Product Vision

## 1. Mission

To eliminate financial anxiety by providing individuals with absolute clarity and control over their money, credit, and debt through a radically simple and unified interface.

## 2. Vision

Finance Tracker is a premium personal finance management application designed for individuals who want precision without complexity. 

Unlike traditional finance apps that overwhelm users with complex accounting jargon, intrusive ads, or forced bank integrations that frequently break, Finance Tracker focuses on manual, intentional tracking. It bridges the gap between daily expenses, credit card utilization, and informal borrowing—presenting a holistic view of financial health in an interface that feels fast, professional, and intuitive. People choose Finance Tracker because it respects their privacy, values their time, and turns financial discipline from a chore into a seamless daily habit.

## 3. Problems We Solve

- **Fragmented Financial Awareness:** Users struggle to understand their true financial position because their cash, credit cards, and informal debts (borrowing from/lending to friends) are tracked in different places or not at all.
- **Credit Card Anxiety:** Without clear visibility into credit utilization, users risk overspending on credit cards, leading to debt cycles and impacted credit scores.
- **Lost Track of Borrowed Money:** Informal lending and borrowing (IOUs) are often forgotten, leading to awkward social situations or lost funds.
- **Lack of Budget Discipline:** Setting a budget is easy; sticking to it requires real-time, frictionless tracking that most apps fail to provide.
- **Complex Interfaces:** Most finance tools feel like spreadsheets. Users abandon them because they require too much mental overhead to log a simple transaction.

## 4. Target Users

**Primary Users**
- Young professionals and individuals actively working to build better financial habits.
- Credit card users who want strict visibility over their monthly utilization and limits.
- People who frequently manage informal debts (splitting bills, lending money to peers).

**Secondary Users**
- Freelancers looking for a simple way to track isolated business expenses without full-scale accounting software.

**Who is NOT the target user?**
- Enterprise businesses requiring double-entry bookkeeping, payroll, and tax compliance.
- Users looking for automated, zero-touch bank syncing and AI-driven investment advice.

## 5. Product Goals

- **Frictionless Entry:** Reduce the time it takes to log a transaction to under 3 seconds.
- **Immediate Clarity:** Ensure users understand their current budget remaining, total spend, and debt status within 5 seconds of opening the application.
- **High Retention:** Achieve a high 30-day retention rate by making the daily logging habit rewarding and effortless.
- **Zero Ambiguity:** Every number on the screen must clearly explain what it represents (no hidden calculations or confusing charts).

## 6. Core Principles

- **Speed First:** The app must open instantly. Inputting data must never feel like a chore.
- **Privacy-First:** User financial data is strictly confidential. No selling data, no third-party financial tracking.
- **Intentional Tracking:** Manual entry forces mindfulness. We prioritize conscious financial awareness over automated apathy.
- **Professional Aesthetics:** The application must feel premium, trustworthy, and calm. Finance is stressful enough; the UI should not be.
- **Simplicity Over Completeness:** We will always choose a clean, understandable interface over cramming in edge-case features.

## 7. MVP Scope (Version 1)

Version 1 focuses on the absolute essentials required to form a daily financial habit.

- **Secure Authentication:** Standard account creation and login.
- **Dashboard:** A clear summary of the current month's total spend, remaining budget, and credit utilization.
- **Expense Tracking:** Fast, manual logging of daily expenses categorized by type and payment method.
- **Category Management:** Ability to create, edit, and delete custom expense categories.
- **Credit Card Management:** Track multiple credit cards, their specific limits, and current usage.
- **Borrowing Tracking (IOUs):** A dedicated interface to log money borrowed or lent, including the source/destination and amount.
- **Monthly Budget Limit:** A simple global setting to cap monthly expenditure targets.

## 8. Future Scope

**Version 2**
- Historical trends and monthly comparison reports.
- Data export capabilities (CSV/PDF) for personal archiving.
- Advanced filtering and search for past transactions.

**Version 3 and Beyond**
- Collaborative budgets (shared households).
- Customizable budget limits per specific category, rather than just a global limit.
- Receipt scanning for faster manual entry.

## 9. Success Metrics

- **Daily Active Users (DAU) to Monthly Active Users (MAU) Ratio:** Indicates habit formation.
- **Time to Log:** Average duration from app launch to successful transaction entry.
- **Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who set up a budget and log at least 10 transactions in their first week.
- **Crash-Free Sessions:** Ensuring the application remains reliable and trustworthy.

## 10. Non-Goals

The following features are explicitly out of scope for Version 1, and potentially forever, to maintain product focus:

- Automated bank account linking and Plaid integrations.
- Stock market tracking, crypto portfolios, or investment advice.
- Bill splitting calculators (we track the result, we don't do the math for the dinner table).
- Gamification (badges, points, or leaderboards). Financial clarity is the reward.
